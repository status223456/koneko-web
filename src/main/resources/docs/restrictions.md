# Account restrictions

A restriction is a timeout from the community placed on accounts that show abnormal, suspicious or
rule-breaking behaviour.

While an account is restricted it cannot interact with the community. Restricted players can still
play the game, download beatmaps and submit scores, but their profile is invisible to everyone else
and their scores are visible only to themselves.

The following is disabled for a restricted account:

- chatting in game and on the website
- appearing on any leaderboard
- taking part in multiplayer rooms
- being visible on the player list and on profiles

## My account has been restricted! What do I do? {id=what}

Do not panic, and please follow these steps:

- **Wait 24 hours.** Most restrictions are the result of an automated process. Every new restriction
  is reviewed, so if yours was a mistake it will normally be lifted within a day.
- **Ask on our Discord server.** If your account is still restricted after that, join our
  [Discord server]({{discord}}) and open a ticket there. Write from the account you want to discuss,
  and include your username together with anything you can tell us about why you think the
  restriction happened. The more you tell us, the faster it can be sorted out.
- If you are convinced the restriction is a mistake, ask on the Discord server straight away so we
  can look at your case.

If your account was restricted for breaking the rules, there is a waiting period before an appeal is
considered. This is called a *cooldown*.

## Why am I restricted? {id=why}

There are several reasons an account can be restricted.

- **It is not your first account.** Multi-accounting is against the rules. Accounts other than your
  first one are restricted, while the original one is usually left open so you can keep playing on
  it.
- **You let somebody else play on your account.** Account sharing is against the rules as well. If
  it happened by accident, tell us on the Discord server as soon as you can.
- **You cheated.** Third-party programs, macros or any other unfair advantage lead to an immediate
  restriction.
- **You kept misbehaving.** Players who are repeatedly silenced for breaking the rules in chat or
  anywhere else on the server are eventually restricted.
- **Other cases.** The staff may restrict an account for reasons that are not on this list.

## What do I do while I wait? {id=while-waiting}

- **Keep playing.** You are welcome to keep playing on your original account while restricted. Your
  scores are still submitted and stay on the account once the restriction is lifted, unless the
  account is rolled back.
- **Behave.** Breaking more rules while restricted resets the cooldown before you may appeal.
- **Do not make another account.** Creating a new account to keep playing online counts as evading
  the restriction and makes things considerably worse.
- **Ask if you are unsure.** As long as you are not trying to get around the punishment, we are
  happy to answer questions about your restriction on the Discord server.

## How do I appeal? {id=appeal}

Appeals are handled on our [Discord server]({{discord}}). Open a ticket with the subject
`Restriction appeal: <username>` and include:

- **Your account history.** Every rule you broke: other accounts, account sharing, cheating,
  silences. You do not need exact dates, you do need to be honest.
- **Why it happened.** An explanation of what led you to do it helps us judge whether it is likely to
  happen again.
- **Your username** and the approximate date the restriction started.

After you send an appeal, expect an answer within two weeks. If nothing has come by then, follow up
in the same ticket.

## What if my appeal is granted? {id=appeal-granted}

If we decide to let you back in, you will be asked to agree to some terms first:

- **Play on one account only**, the one that has been unrestricted.
- **No further cheating, sharing or multi-accounting.** Any of them ends the second chance.
- **Any major misbehaviour results in an immediate restriction again.**

## My appeal has been denied {id=appeal-denied}

The usual reasons are:

- **You appealed too early.** The cooldown for your case has not passed yet.
- **You were evading the restriction.** Playing on other accounts while restricted resets the
  cooldown from the day of the last offence.
- **You kept breaking the rules.**
- **Your history is too severe.** In rare cases we may decide that the account will not be returning.

Each further restriction on the same account roughly doubles the length of the next cooldown.

## Restricted again right after unrestriction {id=re-restricted}

Some players are restricted again by the automated systems shortly after their appeal is granted.
This is normal and is usually undone within 24 hours. Say so on the
[Discord server]({{discord}}) and it will be dealt with faster.

## What to include when you ask us {id=what-to-include}

- Your username, and the user id if you know it
- How long the account has been restricted
- The main reason you believe it was restricted
- Anything else you think we should know

## Common reasons and cooldowns {id=reasons}

| Reason | Cooldown | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Automated flag | Up to 24 hours | Usually lifted by itself after review. |
| Multi-accounting | Permanent on the extra accounts | The original account normally stays open. |
| Account sharing | 3 months | Tell us early if it was an accident. |
| Cheating | 6 months | Doubles with every further offence. |
| Repeated silences | 3 months | Behaviour is reviewed as a whole. |
